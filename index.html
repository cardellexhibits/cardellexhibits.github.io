Services

Service for the interactives already on your floor.

01

Survey

A half-day on site, followed by a station-by-station punch list within five days. Each interactive gets a clear recommendation: leave it, schedule a callout, or plan a conversion.

02

Callout

On-site diagnosis and repair for an existing interactive that does not need a conversion.

03

Conversion

Keep the exhibit and replace the aging player or control hardware with serviceable, cord-and-plug components, backup media, and a clear operator reference.

04

Care

Quarterly checks for a named list of interactives over a one-year term. Parts and conversions are handled separately.

No gallery remodels, new fabrication, or display sales. For television and projector work, Cardell refers to Desco AV in Olympia.
