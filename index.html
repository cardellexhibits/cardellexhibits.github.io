Request a survey View services

Services

Technical service for existing interactive exhibits.

01

Survey

Following a half-day site visit, Cardell provides a station-by-station punch list within five days. Each station receives a recommendation for continued use, a callout, or conversion.

02

Callout

On-site diagnosis and repair for existing interactive exhibits when conversion is not required.

03

Conversion

Retain the existing exhibit while aging player or control hardware is replaced with serviceable, cord-and-plug components, backup media, and operator documentation.

04

Care

Quarterly service checks for a named list of interactive exhibits under a one-year term. Parts and conversions are provided separately.

Cardell Exhibit Service does not provide gallery remodeling, new fabrication, display sales, or television and projector service. Television and projector inquiries are referred to Desco AV in Olympia.
