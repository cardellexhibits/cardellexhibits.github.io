Services

Technical service for existing interactive exhibits.

01

Survey

Following a half-day site visit, Cardell provides a station-by-station punch list within five days. The punch list identifies whether each station stays in service, needs a callout, or should be converted.

02

Callout

On-site diagnosis and repair for existing interactive exhibits when conversion is not required.

03

Conversion

Retain the existing exhibit while aging player or control hardware is replaced with serviceable, cord-and-plug components, backup media, and operator documentation.

04

Care

Quarterly service checks for a named list of interactive exhibits under a one-year term. Parts and conversions are provided separately.

Process

From survey to approved work.

  1. 01

    Survey

    A half-day site visit reviews the condition and intended operation of each station.

  2. 02

    Punch list

    Within five days, Cardell delivers a station-by-station plan: leave in service, callout, or convert.

  3. 03

    Callout / Conversion / Care

    Approved work moves forward as a repair, conversion, or scheduled quarterly care.

Why Cardell

  • Document the work.
  • Leave serviceable components and clear operator guidance.
